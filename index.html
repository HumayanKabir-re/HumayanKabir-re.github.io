Featured Projects

Live

Mercato

A multilingual retrieval architecture, currently demonstrated across English and Japanese, over the Amazon ESCI dataset. English queries run three retrieval channels in parallel (BM25 keyword, bge-m3 dense semantic, CLIP image); Japanese queries run two. Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges the ranked lists and a per-locale LambdaMART model reranks the fused pool. Evaluated on the complete benchmark splits with significance testing, and deployed on CPU across two free-tier clouds.

Neural Cryptanalysis of SHA-256

A research study of whether learned models can recover structure from SHA-256's round function. Five independent method families were evaluated across six phases, with multi-seed runs, bootstrap confidence intervals, and capacity ablations. A Speck32/64 neural distinguisher served as a positive control to confirm the method finds structure where structure exists. All five families converged on the same limit at round five.

ESCI-JP

An open image-embedding extension for the Japanese locale of the ESCI dataset. No public dataset provides Japanese image coverage equivalent to SQID's English coverage, which is why Japanese search in Mercato runs text only and its reranker has two fewer features. Follows SQID's methodology and license, distributing embeddings and references rather than images.
